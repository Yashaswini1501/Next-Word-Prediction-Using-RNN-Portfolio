Problem Statement

Why predict the next word at all?

Every autocomplete, chatbot reply, and search suggestion is a bet on what word comes next. Get that bet right, consistently, and typing feels like it's reading your mind.

✎

Where this shows up

Search engines, chatbots, virtual assistants, and text editors all lean on next-word prediction to power autocomplete, sentence completion, and on-the-fly text generation.

⟲

What the model has to learn

Words don't carry meaning in isolation — "fast" after "very" and "car" means something different than "fast" after "charging." The model has to learn that context from word order alone.

▲

The objective: build a neural language model that learns contextual relationships between words from a small text corpus — using word embeddings and a SimpleRNN — and predicts the most probable next word for any seed sequence, recursively, to generate full sentences.

Corpus

Five sentences, no CSV

There's no dataset file here — the training signal is a small, hand-written corpus of smartphone-themed sentences, expanded into every partial prefix the model could see.

Pipeline

Nine steps, corpus to generated sentence

Run top to bottom in the notebook — each step's output feeds the next.

01

Define the corpus

A handful of hand-written sentences stand in for a dataset — no CSV or external file required.

02

Tokenization

Keras' Tokenizer fits on the corpus and maps every unique word to an integer index.

03

N-gram sequence generation

Each sentence is exploded into every partial prefix, so the model sees "short → long" context growth.

04

Padding

pad_sequences left-pads every sequence to the longest sentence's length so batches align.

05

Feature / label split

All tokens but the last become X; the last token becomes the one-hot label y.

06

Model construction

Embedding → SimpleRNN(64) → Dense(softmax) over the vocabulary.

07

Training

Compiled with Adam and categorical crossentropy, trained for 300 epochs on the full corpus.

08

Next-word prediction

A seed phrase is tokenized, padded, and passed through the model; argmax picks the most likely word.

09

Recursive generation

The predicted word is appended to the seed and fed back in, looped next_words times to write a sentence.
